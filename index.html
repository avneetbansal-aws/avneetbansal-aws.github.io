About

I am a Delivery Consultant at Amazon Web Services Professional Services, where I build cloud-native platforms and Generative AI systems for enterprise customers. My work sits where Kubernetes, infrastructure as code, and large language models meet production.

Most recently I designed an agentic contract-intelligence platform on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore. It routes each extracted field to the right model at runtime, heals itself from human corrections, and cut per-document processing cost by about 75%. The platform received the CV-S3 2026 AI-Powered Automation Excellence Award.

Before AWS, I spent over a decade in build, release, and DevOps engineering for Universal Studios, NBCUniversal, Ford, and Ygrene Energy Fund. I contribute upstream to CNCF and Kubernetes SIGs projects such as KEDA, Argo, Harbor, and external-dns.
